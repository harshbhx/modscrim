## Still stuck?

- **Found a bug?** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/harshbhx/modscrim/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Say which surface (console, mod view, overlay, report, demo or website), what you expected and what happened.
- **Have an idea?** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/harshbhx/modscrim/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security problem?** Do not open an issue. Follow the [Security policy](SECURITY.md).

Never paste a Twitch token, a secret or real chat text into an issue.

ModScrim is a hackathon project maintained by two people, so replies are best effort.
