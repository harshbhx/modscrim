How a drill runs

About six minutes from the first whistle to a report. Your mods practise on fake accounts in a chat only they can see, and your filters get tested against attacks they have never seen.

  1. 0:00

    Pick a play

    Choose the tactics: fake giveaway links, follower and viewer spam, art and promo bots. Set the length, the speed and how long a line can sit before it slips through.

  2. 0:30

    First whistle

    Your mods join from any device with a 6-digit code. Every screen says practice, and your viewers see a safe overlay with the attack text blurred out.

  3. 0:35

    The raid lands

    A line every second and a half, mixed with harmless banter. Mods delete, time out or ban. The first action wins; a line nobody touches for 9 seconds slips through.

  4. 2:00

    Final whistle

    Each mod gets caught, false bans and average reaction time, and the reveal shows which lines were attacks and which tricks they used.

  5. 2:30

    Twitch grades your filters

    Every attack line goes through Twitch’s Check AutoMod Status: “9 of 14 attack lines would get past your filters.”

  6. 3:30

    Fix the rules

    Up to six blocked terms that catch what got past, each checked against last week’s real chat so it can’t ban a regular.

  7. 4:30

    Prove it on a fresh wave

    A new wave built with tricks the rule finder never saw. You see blocked before and after, and real-chat matches, which must be zero.

  8. 5:00

    Approve and push

    Nothing reaches Twitch until you press the button. Undo removes every term the drill pushed.

House rules

ModScrim is a practice tool. These rules keep practice from ever touching the real thing.

Read the full safety page

  • Practice stays in practice

    Generated attacks only appear in the practice chat. ModScrim never posts them to Twitch, and every link ends in .invalid, so it can’t open.

  • Viewers see a safe overlay

    The OBS overlay shows usernames, outcomes and the scoreboard, with every attack message drawn as a blurred bar. Viewers never learn a working trick.

  • You push every change

    No term reaches your channel until you press Approve, and every push has an Undo. ModScrim reads your AutoMod settings and never changes them.

  • Your chat log stays yours

    Kept for 7 days, used only to check that new rules don’t ban your regulars, never shown on stream, and deleted when you ask or sign out.

  • Not affiliated with Twitch

    ModScrim is an independent tool that signs in with Twitch. It isn’t made or endorsed by Twitch.
