How a drill runs

About six minutes from the first whistle to a report. Your mods practise on fake accounts in a chat only they can see.

  1. Practise

    Pick the tactics, share a link and a 6-digit code, and your mods clear the practice chat. Each gets caught, false bans and reaction time.

  2. Grade

    Every attack line goes through Twitch’s Check AutoMod Status, so you see how many would get past your filters.

  3. Fix

    Up to six blocked terms that catch what got past, each checked against your last 7 days of real chat.

  4. Prove and push

    A fresh wave shows blocked before and after, with real-chat matches that must be zero. Then you approve, and Undo is one click.

Built so practice never touches the real thing

ModScrim puts realistic attacks on screen during a live stream. These rules keep that safe. Read the safety page

  • Practice stays in practice

    Generated attacks only appear in the practice chat. ModScrim never posts them to Twitch, and every link ends in .invalid, so it can’t open.

  • Viewers see a safe overlay

    The OBS overlay shows usernames, outcomes and the scoreboard, with every attack message drawn as a blurred bar. Viewers never learn a working trick.

  • You push every change

    No term reaches your channel until you press Approve, and every push has an Undo. ModScrim reads your AutoMod settings and never changes them.

  • Graded by Twitch

    The headline numbers come from Twitch’s own Check AutoMod Status. Where Twitch’s hourly limit runs out, the number says “local estimate” instead.

  • Your chat log stays yours

    Kept for 7 days, used only to check that new rules don’t ban your regulars, never shown on stream, and deleted when you ask or sign out.

  • Open source, not Twitch

    MIT licensed, with the code in a public repository. ModScrim is an independent tool that signs in with Twitch. It isn’t made or endorsed by Twitch.

Common questions

The short answers. See all questions

Does ModScrim post anything to my chat?

No. Attacks only exist in the practice chat. The one optional exception is a single fixed line when a drill starts: “ModScrim practice drill starting. Practice chat only, nothing here is real.” It’s off unless you turn it on.

Is the grading real?

On a real channel, yes. Twitch’s Check AutoMod Status grades every line it can within your channel’s hourly limit. Anything graded by ModScrim’s own matcher is labelled “local estimate”, and demo rooms always use the local estimate.

What if a new term bans a regular?

ModScrim rejects any term that matches your last 7 days of chat, and push is disabled unless real-chat matches are zero. If one still causes trouble later, Undo this push removes it, or you can remove it in your Twitch dashboard.

How much does it cost?

ModScrim is a free, open-source hackathon project. It runs on Cloudflare’s free tier and makes no paid AI calls.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You, your mods and OBS all use links.
