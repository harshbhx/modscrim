Mod docs. You moderate a channel that runs drills and want to know what you will see and do. Not what you came for? Find the right docs.

The practice chat always shows the banner “Practice chat only. Nothing here is posted to Twitch.” Lines arrive every second or so: attacks written to slip past your channel’s own filters, mixed with harmless banter from a fixed pool.

The three actions

ActionKeyWhat it does in a drill
DeleteDRemoves the focused line
TimeoutTTimes out its sender
BanBBans its sender

Every action also has a button. All three only score the drill: nobody real is removed from anything.

If two mods act on the same line

Whoever reached the server first gets it. The other mod sees “Sam got it first” (with that mod’s name).

What each line tells you

  • Banned by you · 2.1 s: you caught an attack, and how long you took. (The seconds shown are an example.)
  • False ban: that was a regular: you acted on a harmless line.
  • Slipped through: nobody acted within the slip window.
