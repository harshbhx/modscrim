KeysWhat it does
↑ ↓Move between open lines
Home EndJump to the first or last open line
DDelete the focused line
TTime out its sender
BBan its sender
?Show the keyboard help

The same actions are available as buttons on every line, which is what you use on a phone. Score changes and slipped lines are announced politely to screen readers.
