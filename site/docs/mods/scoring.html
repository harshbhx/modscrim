Mod docs. You moderate a channel that runs drills and want to know what you will see and do. Not what you came for? Find the right docs.

Caught
You acted on an attack line.
False ban
You acted on a banter line, which was a regular. It is shown as “False ban: that was a regular”.
Slipped
An attack line nobody removed within the slip window (9 seconds by default; your streamer can set 5 to 15). Banter never slips. Slipped lines are what the filters need to catch.
Reaction time
How long you took, from when the line appeared to when you acted.

The first action wins

If two mods act on the same line, whoever reached the server first gets the credit.

At the end

You see your caught, false bans and average reaction time, the team scoreboard, and the reveal: which lines were attacks and which tricks they used. The reveal is for the console and mods; it is never shown on the overlay.

Today each drill has its own report; a mod-history view across drills is planned.
