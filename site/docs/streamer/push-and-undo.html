Streamer docs. You run a Twitch channel and want your mods ready for a raid. Not what you came for? Find the right docs.

Approve and push

In step 6, Approve and push adds the terms you ticked in step 5 to your channel’s blocked terms on Twitch. The button is disabled unless the proposed terms match none of your real chat from the last 7 days. No term reaches Twitch without your explicit approval.

ModScrim never calls Twitch’s AutoMod settings update, which overwrites every setting at once. It only reads your levels.

Undo this push

Undo this push removes every term that drill’s push added. A term that already existed on Twitch before the push is left alone. You can press it from the console or from that drill’s report. You can also remove any term yourself in your Twitch dashboard.

Remove everything

  • Chat log: press Delete chat log in step 1 at any time, or Pause logging. It is otherwise kept for 7 days.
  • Overlay URL: press Rotate key and old links stop working at once.
  • Everything: Sign out and delete my data removes your tokens, chat log, drills, reports and chat subscription. Removing ModScrim from your Twitch connections does the same once Twitch tells ModScrim about the change.
  • Audit log: pushes, undos, sign-ins, sign-outs, chat-log deletions and overlay key changes are written to your audit log.

See the privacy policy for what is stored and for how long.
