Try a demo room

Run the whole loop without a Twitch account: a private practice room with a sample channel, a simulated co-mod and every step from filters to push.

What you get

  • A sample channel

    pixelpanda_tv, with five public blocked terms and a curated 71-line chat log for the false-ban check.

  • A co-mod who never sleeps

    Kai (sim) acts on about 60 percent of attacks after a few seconds and sometimes falls for tempting banter, so you can see a false ban happen. Kai is always labelled and scored separately.

  • The full loop

    Drill, score, grade, find rules, prove them on a fresh wave and run a simulated push and undo.

  • Grading is a local estimate

    Demo rooms never call Twitch, so grades come from ModScrim’s own matcher and say so. On a real channel, Twitch does the grading.

  • It cleans up after itself

    Your room is private to your browser and deletes itself two hours after its last activity.
