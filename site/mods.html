For mods

Practise on fake raids without an audience, get a score, and get faster. You join from any device with a link and a 6-digit code.

Your part in a drill

  1. 1

    Open the mod link

    Your streamer shares a link that ends in /mod/ and their channel name. Type the 6-digit code and the name you want on the scoreboard.

  2. 2

    Wait for the whistle

    “You’re in. Remove attacks, leave regulars alone.” You’ll see who else has joined.

  3. 3

    Clear the practice chat

    Delete, time out or ban each attack. Leave banter alone: acting on a regular counts as a false ban. If two mods act on the same line, whoever reached the server first gets it.

  4. 4

    See your score

    Caught, false bans and your average reaction time, the team scoreboard, and the reveal: which lines were attacks and which tricks they used.

Good to know

  • It’s opt-in

    You only see the practice chat when you join a drill, and it always carries the “Practice chat only” banner.

  • Your actions don’t touch Twitch

    Delete, time out and ban only score the drill. Nobody real is removed from anything.

  • Your name on stream is your streamer’s call

    The overlay can show “Mod 1”, “Mod 2” instead of names. Ask your streamer to switch it on if you’d rather stay anonymous.

  • Kai is not a person

    Kai (sim) is a simulated co-mod for rehearsing alone. It’s always labelled and scored separately.
