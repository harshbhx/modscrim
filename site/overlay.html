What’s on screen

  • The practice-drill bar, so nobody mistakes it for a real raid.
  • The practice chat with readable usernames, blurred messages and outcomes.
  • The scoreboard: caught, false bans and average reaction per mod, and the team’s slipped count.
  • After the drill, an after-action card for 20 seconds with the Twitch grade, terms pushed and the fresh-wave result.

What never is

  • Attack text. The server sends the overlay only the length of each line.
  • The reveal of tricks, or anything from your chat log.
  • Mod names, if you turn on Hide mod names.
  • Anything at all with a wrong or rotated key.
