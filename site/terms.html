Using ModScrim

These terms apply when you use the ModScrim website and app run by Harsh Bhanushali and Dhairya Patel (“we”). By signing in, joining a drill or opening a demo room, you agree to them. If you use ModScrim for a team or organisation, you confirm you can accept these terms for it.

You must be old enough to use Twitch where you live and follow Twitch’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

What ModScrim does

ModScrim is a practice tool. It runs simulated raids in a practice chat that only your mods can see, scores your team, asks Twitch to grade your filters, proposes blocked terms and, when you approve, adds them to your channel with an undo. It doesn’t moderate your real chat, and it isn’t a replacement for AutoMod, moderation bots or your own judgement.

Grades, proposed terms and fresh-wave results are estimates of how your filters behave. Twitch’s systems can change, and two engines can disagree. A term that passed every check can still affect messages you didn’t expect.

Your responsibilities

  • You decide what to push. You are responsible for the blocked terms you approve and for removing any that cause problems.
  • Tell your community that your channel’s chat is logged for 7 days for the false-ban check, for example in your panels or rules.
  • Only invite people who have agreed to take part in drills, and respect a mod’s choice not to.
  • Keep your overlay link private, and rotate its key if it leaks.

Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • copy generated attack lines out of the practice chat to post them anywhere, including Twitch chat;
  • use ModScrim to harass anyone, or to build, test or share ways to evade another channel’s moderation;
  • try to read data from channels or rooms that aren’t yours, guess join codes, or get around rate limits;
  • overload, disrupt or reverse-engineer the hosted service beyond what the open-source licence allows;
  • use ModScrim in a way that breaks Twitch’s Terms of Service or Developer Agreement, or the law.

We may suspend access that breaks these rules.
